Corporate Law July 20, 2023

Key Changes in India's 2023 Tax Laws

1. Revised Surcharge Rates

The Finance Act 2023 has reduced the surcharge on income tax from 37% to 25% for individuals earning above ₹5 crores annually. This change affects:

  • High-net-worth individuals
  • Senior professionals
  • Entrepreneurs

2. New TDS Provisions

Section 194R now mandates 10% TDS on benefits/perquisites exceeding ₹20,000 annually:

Example: If a company provides free accommodation worth ₹50,000 to a consultant, ₹5,000 TDS will apply.
