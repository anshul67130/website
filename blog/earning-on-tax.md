## Introduction
Income tax is a direct tax levied by governments on income earned by individuals and businesses. It's a crucial source of revenue that funds public services and infrastructure. In this guide, we'll break down the fundamentals of income tax.

## Types of Income Tax
1. **Individual Income Tax** - Levied on wages, salaries, and other incomes
2. **Corporate Income Tax** - Applied to business profits
3. **Capital Gains Tax** - On profits from asset sales
4. **Self-Employment Tax** - For freelancers and independent contractors
